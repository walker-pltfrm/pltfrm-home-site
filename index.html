00 / Studio About the practice

A small studio building a handful of things, very carefully.

PLTFRM is independent on purpose. Three products in active development across software and hardware, all designed and engineered with the same hand. The aim is work that feels patient: native speed, considered interfaces, almost nothing in the way.

We do not chase trends, ship beta-quality features, or pad the catalog with side projects. Focus is the strategy.

  • P / 01

    Native first.

    Built for Apple platforms, the way Apple apps should feel. Quick to open, quiet to use, never a port from somewhere else.

  • P / 02

    Quiet interface.

    The interface is a frame for the work, not the work itself. Restraint over decoration. Density only when the task demands it.

  • P / 03

    Few things, well.

    One studio, three products. No roadmap of side projects, no growth chart on the wall. Patience as a design principle.

Argument

II. B.Application of the residual exception.

Where a witness's prior inconsistent statement was offered under conditions that did not afford substantial guarantees of trustworthiness, the residual exception cannot rescue an otherwise inadmissible record.

  1. The statement was not sworn under penalty of perjury.
  2. The witness was not subject to contemporaneous cross-examination.
  3. No independent physical evidence corroborates the account
