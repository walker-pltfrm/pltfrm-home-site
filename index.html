Briefcase

Organize Your Legal Arguments

Briefcase is a structured writing tool for law students and legal professionals. Organize briefs, memos, outlines, and class notes with a hierarchical section editor, rich text formatting, and one-click export to DOCX. Built natively for macOS and iOS.

Structured Outline

Hierarchical sections and subsections with drag-and-drop reordering. Organize arguments the way you think.

Rich Text Editing

Bold, italic, underline, small caps, and bullet lists. Format your arguments with precision and style.

DOCX Export

Publish in multiple formats — appellate brief, legal memo, outline, or plain. Export directly to Word-compatible DOCX.

Apple Native

Built with SwiftUI for macOS and iOS. Clean, light interface designed to feel right at home on your Mac.

Briefs

Structure appellate and trial briefs with organized sections for issues, rules, arguments, and authorities.

Memos

Draft legal memoranda with IRAC-structured analysis, complete with TO/FROM headers and formal formatting.

Doc Review Organization

Categorize and annotate documents during review. Tag by importance, status, and category for fast retrieval.

Case Strategy

Map out your case theory with hierarchical arguments, counterarguments, and supporting authorities in one place.

Class Notes

Capture lecture notes with rich formatting and bullet lists. Organize by topic with collapsible sections.

Outlines

Build course outlines and study guides with nested sections, drag-and-drop reordering, and one-click export.
