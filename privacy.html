PLTFRM ("we," "our," "us") built the FFL (Freedom Football League) app. This policy explains what data we touch, what we don't, and how to control it.

§ 01

Information we collect

Game Data

FFL stores your game progress, team rosters, player statistics, and league data locally on your device. This data is created entirely within the app based on your gameplay.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, your game data may be synchronized across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This allows you to continue your game on multiple devices. The data is stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

FFL uses Apple Game Center for multiplayer features and leaderboards. When you use these features, your Game Center nickname and gameplay achievements may be visible to other players. Game Center data is managed by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Multiplayer

When using peer-to-peer multiplayer features, your device may connect directly with other players' devices on the same local network. Only game-related data necessary for multiplayer gameplay is exchanged during these sessions.

§ 02

In-app purchases and subscriptions

FFL is free to download and play. We may introduce optional in-app purchases or subscription features in the future to unlock additional content or functionality. The base game will always remain free. Any purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store and are subject to Apple's terms and conditions. We do not have access to your payment information.

§ 03

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not serve advertisements.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
§ 04

Storage and security

All game data is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, in your personal iCloud account. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Your data is protected by your device's security features and Apple's iCloud security measures.

§ 05

Children's privacy

FFL does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or collection of personal data to play the base game. The app does require users of any age to use or create an iCloud account in order to play this game in its multiplayer mode.

§ 06

Your choices

  • iCloud Sync. You can disable iCloud sync for the app in Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud.
  • Game Center. You can manage Game Center settings in Settings > Game Center.
  • Delete Data. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device.
§ 07

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

§ 08

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at pltfrmsoftware@gmail.com.

This privacy policy applies to the FFL app available on the Apple App Store.
